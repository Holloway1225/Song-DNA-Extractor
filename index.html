How it works
Pick what kind of material you're pasting — song, movie, TV show, or book — then run it through two passes.

Pass 1 — Read and abstract: reads the source literally and emotionally, identifies its setting, posture, central object/action, and core tension, then goes deeper — the emotional wound underneath the literal situation and the central paradox driving it. It also names two fully independent, unrelated analyses: a source engine (the conceptual system, if any, the source itself is genuinely using — purely analytical, shown to you, never sent anywhere else) and a transfer engine (a conceptual system chosen only because its own mechanics fit the wound and paradox — never derived from, mirrored against, or related to the source engine in any way). Either can come back "DIRECT" when nothing forced would help. Shown to you so you can see what the tool is reading from.

Pass 2 — Engineered ChatGPT prompt: receives only the setting, posture, object, tension, wound, paradox, and transfer engine — never the original source text, title, names, or the source engine at all. This is a deliberate abstraction barrier between inspiration and composition: Pass 2 has no way to anchor to the source's vocabulary, cadence, or imagery, because it never sees it, and no way to reference what conceptual system the source used, because it's never told. When the transfer engine is DIRECT, the conceptual-system section is left out of the generated prompt silently — with no note that anything was skipped. It builds the engineered prompt around the wound, the paradox, and (when present) the transfer engine — with rules for emotional progression, an undiscovered hook, an optional human-fracture moment, and originality and restraint requirements — and leaves the actual lines for ChatGPT to discover. It never hands over invented physical details, a pre-written hook, or finished metaphors.

Hard rule for Pass 1: the source's own lines, phrasing, or dialogue are never quoted or reused, even in the structure read. Pass 2 physically cannot reuse them — it never receives them.
Example shape of what Pass 2 hands back: "You are a world-class songwriter. The emotional wound: someone keeps performing belief in something that quietly stopped being true. Governing conceptual system: [a transfer engine — unrelated to whatever the source actually used] — its own vocabulary of obligation, breach, renewal, the gap between what was promised and what was meant. The governing metaphor should not merely decorate the song; it should govern how the song thinks… [emotional progression] … [hook and optional human-fracture requirements] … [Suno tag formatting] … Begin directly with Verse 1. Output only the finished lyrics."
