How it works
Pick what kind of material you're pasting — song, movie, TV show, or book — then run it through two passes.

Concept Reader separates what a song says from how it works. Craft DNA preserves the source's transferable songwriting mechanics while the Transfer Engine gives the new song a different expressive identity.

Pass 1 — Read and abstract: reads the source literally and emotionally, identifies its setting, posture, central object/action, and core tension, then goes deeper — the emotional wound and central paradox (inferring depth without inventing severity), the source engine (the conceptual system, if any, the source itself genuinely uses — purely analytical, shown to you, never sent anywhere else), an independently-chosen transfer engine (a conceptual system picked only because its own mechanics fit the wound and paradox — never derived from or related to the source engine), and Craft DNA — the 3-6 transferable songwriting mechanics that actually make this source work (repetition behavior, withholding, escalation, recognition, and so on), stripped of every trace of its topic, imagery, vocabulary, and genre. Any engine can come back "DIRECT" when nothing forced would help.

Pass 2 — Engineered ChatGPT prompt: receives the emotional abstraction, the transfer engine, and Craft DNA — never the original source text, title, names, or the source engine. This is a deliberate abstraction barrier: Pass 2 can't anchor to the source's wording, and can't even name what conceptual system it used. When the transfer engine is DIRECT, the conceptual-system section is left out silently. The generated prompt turns Craft DNA into behavioral instructions (how the idea should move, withhold, or recontextualize) rather than style imitation — preserve the source's songwriting mechanics, replace its expression — and gives ChatGPT the physics of the conceptual world and the craft pressure to write with, while leaving the actual mappings, hook, and lines for ChatGPT to discover.

Hard rule for Pass 1: the source's own lines, phrasing, or dialogue are never quoted or reused, even in the structure read. Pass 2 physically cannot reuse them — it never receives them.
Example shape of what Pass 2 hands back: "You are a world-class songwriter. The emotional wound: someone keeps performing belief in something that quietly stopped being true. Governing conceptual system: [a transfer engine — unrelated to whatever the source actually used] — its own vocabulary of obligation, breach, renewal, the gap between what was promised and what was meant. The governing metaphor should not merely decorate the song; it should govern how the song thinks… [emotional progression] … [hook and optional human-fracture requirements] … [Suno tag formatting] … Begin directly with Verse 1. Output only the finished lyrics."
